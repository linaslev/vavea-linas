Our story

It all started in Finland in 2019.

Two Erasmus students, met in the middle of a snowy winter. What everyone thought would be nothing more than an “Erasmus romance” quickly became something much bigger.

When the exchange ended, we returned to our respective countries, but our story didn’t. Every evening we called each other, sharing our days, our dreams, and counting down until we could be together again. Distance only made us stronger.

Eventually, Vavea took the leap and moved to Lithuania, where our real adventure began. Together, we’ve built a life we’re incredibly proud of: our first apartment, a move to Vilnius, new careers, new adventures. Step by step, we created a place that feels like home.

Then came one of the happiest days of our lives: we got engaged on 12th of December. But that day became even more unforgettable… because our little puppy was born. She came home with us a couple of months after. Just like that, our family became three.

And now, after all these years, we’re so excited for the next chapter.

We can’t wait to celebrate our wedding with the people we love most. We’d love to tell you the rest of our story, so come and spend a few days with us. We promise there will be plenty of laughter, good food, unforgettable memories… and, of course, lots of cuddles from our one and only Poppy.
